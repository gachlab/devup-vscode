## Or just press F5

Open **Run and Debug** and drop down the configuration picker: there is one `devup: <service>` entry per service, without writing a `launch.json`.

Pick one, press F5, and the same thing happens as from the context menu — the two share one implementation on purpose.

**You will not find a port written anywhere, and that is deliberate.** devup starts a debugged service with `--inspect=0`, so the operating system picks a free port and it is a different one on every restart. A `launch.json` with a port in it would be wrong by the second rebuild. The extension reads the port the daemon reports instead.

Which is also why, when your watch rebuilds and the session drops, it comes back on its own: the extension notices the new port and re-attaches.
